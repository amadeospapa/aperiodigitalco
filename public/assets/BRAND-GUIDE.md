# Aperio Digital — Logo & Brand Kit

**The mark:** A three-line "A" that reads as a tunnel receding to a point of light at its tip — a visual play on *Aperio*, Latin for "I open, I reveal." The job of the agency in one image: opening a business to the light so customers can find it.

## Typography
Wordmark: **Space Grotesk** (Bold for APERIO, Medium tracked-out for DIGITAL). Free on Google Fonts. Raster lockups here use a Helvetica-style fallback since Space Grotesk wasn't available at render time — regenerate from the SVG once you have the font for a perfect match.

## Files

**Use these most:**
- `aperio-icon.svg` / `aperio-icon-transparent.svg` — master vector icon, scales to any size, best for web & favicon.
- `aperio-lockup-dark.png` — primary horizontal logo (dark background).
- `aperio-lockup-light.png` — for white/printed backgrounds.
- `favicon.ico` — website tab icon.

**Icon PNGs:**
- `aperio-icon-dark-[32–1024].png` — glow icon on navy square (social profile photos, etc.)
- `aperio-icon-glow-transparent-[32–1024].png` — glow icon, transparent (place on dark surfaces only)
- `aperio-appicon-rounded-[256–1024].png` — rounded app-icon style
- `aperio-icon-light-[256–1024].png` — solid version for white backgrounds (no glow)

## Usage notes
- The **glow versions only work on dark backgrounds.** On white or print, use the **light** (solid-core) versions — the glow is invisible on white.
- For **Google Business Profile**, upload `aperio-icon-dark-512.png` (GBP wants a square logo, min 250×250).
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the light core.
- Don't stretch, recolor the blue, or add a drop shadow — the glow is the only "effect" the brand uses.
